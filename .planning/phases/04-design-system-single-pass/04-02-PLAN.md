<objective>
Align the OS screens with the PDV screens on semantic colour and button discipline (D-05/D-06), and
in doing so eliminate every raw Tailwind palette class from the four highest-drift files in the
app: App.tsx, ServiceOrderKanban.tsx, QuotesCrmScreen.tsx and ui/Button.tsx.

Purpose: UI-SPEC's research found PosScreen.tsx is 100 percent token-compliant while the OS-side
budget-decision UI and the Kanban overdue marker hand-roll the same semantic meanings from raw
palette classes. That asymmetry is the concrete, evidenced OS-vs-PDV divergence D-05 asks this
phase to close. A direct grep of the live tree during planning also found QuotesCrmScreen.tsx
carrying 11 lines of the identical drift — it is a near-clone of the budget-decision card and was
not in UI-SPEC's sample, so it is folded in here rather than left as the one screen that still
misses the design system.

Output: one shared success Button variant, and four files with zero raw palette classes.
</objective>
