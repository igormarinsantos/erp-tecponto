<objective>
Restructure the check-in wizard from 5 steps to 7 (D-03) and land the two locked token-drift
fixes (D-02b), proving the design-system pass end-to-end on the single most structurally involved
change in the phase.

Purpose: the wizard restructuring is the only Phase 4 change that touches state-machine structure
rather than className strings. Proving it first — a real attendant walking a real check-in through
7 steps and creating an identical OS payload — de-risks everything that follows. The two locked
drift fixes (D-02b) ride along because one of them lives in this same file, and together they
establish the raw-palette-to-token substitution pattern that plans 02 and 03 expand.

Output: a 7-step check-in wizard with per-sub-step validation, token-backed BadgeStatus and
ColorChip, and a build-time guard that makes the step-array invariant permanent.
</objective>
