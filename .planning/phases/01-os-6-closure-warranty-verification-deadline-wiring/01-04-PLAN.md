<objective>
Put the deadline in front of the people who need it: pre-filled with a real suggestion where the
técnico sets it, and displayed on the Kanban card and the OS detail overview where everyone else
reads it.

Purpose: plan 01-01 opened the write path with a bare input, and plan 01-03 built the suggestion
backend and proved the customer-facing surfaces. What is left is the operator-facing half of
DEADLINE-01 and DEADLINE-02 — the técnico gets a starting point instead of a blank box, and the
board and the detail view stop being the only places where the promised date is invisible.

Output: the suggestion type and client method, the pre-fill behaviour with its loading and empty
states, a "Prazo estimado" row on the Kanban card, a "Prazo estimado" row in the OS detail
overview, and a frontend-source marker check in the Python suite so these rows cannot silently
disappear.
</objective>
